Winterthur Marathon

24h Comic Challenge

The 24h Comic Challenge in Winterthur is a creative marathon where participants have exactly 24 hours to conceive, write, and draw a complete 24-page comic from scratch.

This gallery showcases five completed stories created during the event, presented in a continuous vertical webtoon format:

  • Light • 24 Pages of silent figures and desert illumination
  • From Nobody to Somebody • 25 Pages of an aspiring young aviator's quest
  • Yeogi & Jeogi • 24 Pages of an alien traveler seeking home across deep divides
  • Still Feels Right • 24 Pages exploring an unbreakable passion for basketball
  • Our Bunnies Sorolla & Aalto • 24 Pages following the endearing journey of two adopted rabbits
